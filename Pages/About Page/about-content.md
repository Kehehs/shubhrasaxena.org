## Editorial Note (read before building)

The homepage content rules mandate calm third person throughout, with credentials stated once and no first-person voice. Your raw draft is written entirely in first person, in a more personal, testimonial register than the homepage.

For the About page, this treatment is used:

- **Framing copy** (section intros, transitions, the podcast section setup) stays in third person, matching the First Light voice and the "philosophical weight over influencer style" rule.
- **The core narrative** (Sections 2 to 4) is kept in first person, presented as her own words, set in Cormorant Garamond italic as a sustained first-person passage rather than homepage-style body copy. This is a deliberate choice: an About page is the one place where hearing her voice directly earns its place, and it distinguishes this page from the homepage instead of repeating its register.

Two lines from your draft were softened, both for the political-safety and 30–55 audience protection rules already established for her, since she is a serving IAS officer and this is the page most likely to be read closely:

1. *"These 3 emotions are indeed the root of all evil"* → *"These three things are the root of much of the suffering I have seen"*. "Root of all evil" reads as a sweeping theological claim rather than a personal observation, which sits oddly next to her institutional role.
2. *"Do you want to defeat the darkness that has engulfed our hearts, souls and minds? Then join me on my mission!"* → reworded into a quieter invitation. The original reads as evangelical recruitment language, which is a tonal mismatch with the calm, dignified register established for her and carries some risk for a civil servant's public page.

Everything else is close to your original, lightly smoothed for flow. Flag if you want either softened line restored closer to the original.
